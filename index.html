I'm Trishan, an undergrad in the final year of my B.Tech in Electrical Engineering at IIT Delhi.

At IIT Delhi, I'm an undergraduate researcher in the LCS2 Lab, where I work with Prof. Tanmoy Chakraborty on temporal consistency in agentic LLMs and multi-agent collaboration for long-horizon tasks.

Before this, I worked at IBM Research on storage-efficient multimodal embedding models, and with Prof. Dianbo Liu at the ASI Team (formerly CogAI4Sci), NUS Singapore, on latent reasoning in LLMs.

Broadly, I'm interested in LLMs, agentic systems and AI safety.
