Hi! I'm Trishan, a final-year undergraduate at IIT Delhi, and I'm interested in LLMs, agentic systems and AI safety.

Currently working on temporal consistency in agentic LLMs and on multi-agent collaboration for long-horizon tasks, with Prof. Tanmoy Chakraborty at IIT Delhi.

Previously, I was a research intern at IBM Research, where I built ColSNAP, a method that cuts the storage cost of visual document retrievers by up to 69×. Before that, I worked on latent reasoning in LLMs with Prof. Dianbo Liu at the Artificial Scientific Intelligence (ASI) Team (formerly CogAI4Sci), NUS Singapore.
